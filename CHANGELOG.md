## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- Controller pictures (Xbox Series, DualSense, Switch Pro, Steam Deck) in one
  line-art style, with measured button anchors and automatic detection.
- Bindings: base + hold-LT + hold-RT layers applied as override bindings, queued
  during combat.
- Profiles: account-wide, per character and per specialization.
- Share strings (`!GG1!`) to export and safely import profiles.
- Slash commands `/gg` and `/grimoire`.
- Cheat sheet card: controller picture with leader lines to every button, peek
  on held LT/RT, pin with LT+R3 or `/gg show`.
- `/gg capture`, `/gg export` / `/gg import` (copy box), `/gg diag`.
- In-game editing on the pinned card: drag a spell, item or macro onto a row,
  right-click to clear, click a tab to switch layer.
